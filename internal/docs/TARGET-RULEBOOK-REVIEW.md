# Target rulebook review

Reviewed 2026-09-08 using the four supplied PDFs and the BDS target appendix they reference.
The visible published catalogue now contains 12 BDS and 12 DSB records. ISSF and NRA catalogues
are no longer offered. Existing personal target records are preserved.

## BDS

- [General part, June 2026](https://www.bdsnet.de/ressourcen/downloads/2026_06_shb_a_final_x.pdf):
  A 9.02 on PDF page 22 confirms higher-ring scoring when the hole visibly touches its boundary.
  A 9.03 and A 9.05 describe tie resolution and excess hits, which remain outside the photo preview.
- [Handguns, July 2026](https://www.bdsnet.de/ressourcen/downloads/2026_07_shb_k_final_x.pdf):
  K 3.04 on PDF page 14 distinguishes the Z 9 precision target from the Z 20 interval/time-series
  target. K 4.04 uses the handgun face for multi-distance shooting. Falling plates and speed
  events require different scoring models and are not represented as ordinary ring scores.
- [Long guns, July 2026](https://www.bdsnet.de/ressourcen/downloads/2026_07_shb_l_final_x.pdf):
  L 2.01, L 2.02 and L 6.01 identify the precision targets. L 1.20 specifies two shots per bull
  for Z 2 and Z 19; L 1.21 specifies one per bull for Z 18. The app displays one bull at a time.
  L 7.04 and L 7.07 distinguish Z 21 precision from Z 9 for the combination stage.
- [Target appendix, revision 30.06.2025](https://www.bdsnet.de/ressourcen/downloads/2025_06_30_shb_z_2025-klein.pdf):
  still the dimensional source for the referenced Z codes. It is cited separately from the 2026
  discipline rules rather than relabelled as a 2026 drawing.

The catalogue includes Z 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 18, 19, 20 and 21. Z 3, 5 and 7 are ISSF-pattern
faces adopted by BDS and cited to the BDS appendix, not entries from a separate ISSF catalogue.
Z 20's nine-ring diameter is 180 mm although the black aiming area is 200 mm. Its outer 10 mm
of black belongs to the eight, as shown by the appendix's scoring illustration. Scoring now has
a regression test for this distinction. Reduced faces Z 16/17 and non-ring targets remain deferred.

## DSB

The [supplied Sportordnung](https://dsb.de/fileadmin/dsb/sportordnung/files/assets/common/downloads/Sportordnung_DSB_01012027.pdf?uni=089ad2d4b106c1ce5c460db31e0ab30b)
is explicitly dated **01.01.2027**, a future edition relative to this review. Records display that
effective date. The former 2014 scan references have been replaced by this PDF's text layer.

Dimensions were checked in 0.20, PDF pages 80-86, against the overview and individual face tables.
The catalogue includes faces 1-8 and 10, with separate records where distance or scoring differs.
Face 4's card range is represented by the permitted 550 × 550 mm format, removing the former
unsourced 535 mm midpoint. The air rifle inner ten remains unevaluated because it requires the
white dot to be completely removed, rather than ordinary ring-touch evaluation.

Rule 0.11.3 on PDF page 57 specifies ordinary ring-touch scoring with exceptions. Rule 1.8.5 on
PDF page 130 and 7.7.5 on PDF page 346 require hole-centre scoring for ordnance and muzzleloader
disciplines. The ordnance and musket records now use centre scoring, with boundary tests.

These are reference diagrams and estimated ring scores. Match penalties, full multi-bull layouts,
decimal scoring, special gauges, moving targets and overlapping photo holes remain separate work.
